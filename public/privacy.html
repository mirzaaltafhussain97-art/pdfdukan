Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2026 · Last Updated: November 10, 2026

PDFdukan processes the files you add to its tools inside your own browser, so your documents are not uploaded to our servers. This policy explains the small amount of other data the site does handle: analytics after you accept the cookie notice, optional sign-in, contact messages and browser storage.

1. What happens to the files I add to a tool?

Files you add to a PDFdukan tool are opened and processed by your browser on your device. The PDF engines, such as pdf.js, pdf-lib, qpdf and Tesseract OCR, run as JavaScript or WebAssembly in the page, so document contents are not sent to PDFdukan.

A file leaves your device only when you choose an action that says so, such as Save to Google Drive, which uploads that output to your own Drive.

2. What information does PDFdukan receive?

PDFdukan receives information only from the features below, and only when you use them.

  • Analytics: after you tap “Got it” on the cookie notice, Google Analytics 4 records page views and tool use, with IP anonymization turned on.
  • Account: if you sign in, Google Firebase Authentication handles your name, email address and sign-in status.
  • Contact messages: your name, email, subject and message reach us by email so we can reply.
  • Tool ratings: if you rate a tool with the stars, we save the score with a one-way scrambled code made from your IP address and browser, so one visitor counts once. The IP address itself is not stored.
  • Server logs: our web host records normal request data such as IP address, browser type, page address and time.

3. Does PDFdukan show ads or sell data?

PDFdukan does not run advertising at the moment and does not sell personal data. Some layouts reserve space for ads; before any ad network is switched on, this policy and the cookie notice will be updated.

4. How do Google Sign-In, Google Drive and Dropbox work?

Google Sign-In and Drive saving are optional. When you connect Drive, PDFdukan asks only for the drive.file permission, which limits access to files the app creates or that you open with it. PDFdukan saves outputs into a folder named “PDFdukan — CamMaster Files”. You can remove access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

If you open a file with the Google Drive or Dropbox button on a tool, that service shows its own file picker. Only the file you choose is downloaded straight into your browser and handed to the tool; it does not pass through PDFdukan’s servers. Dropbox handles that step under the Dropbox Privacy Policy.

5. What does PDFdukan store in my browser?

PDFdukan keeps settings such as theme, language, favorite tools and recent file names in your browser’s local storage; file contents are not kept there. The full list is in our Cookies and Local Storage Policy, and clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

6. Which third parties are involved?

Pages load code libraries from public CDNs such as cdnjs and jsDelivr, which see ordinary request data like your IP address. Google provides Analytics, Firebase Authentication and Drive under the Google Privacy Policy. Contact messages may be delivered through the FormSubmit email service if our own mail service is unavailable.

7. How long is data kept?

Files are never stored by PDFdukan, because they stay in your browser. Contact emails are kept as long as needed to answer you. Analytics data follows the retention setting in Google Analytics, and account records stay until you ask us to delete them.

8. What are my rights?

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, such as your account or a contact message. Send the request through our contact page; please don’t attach sensitive documents.

9. Is PDFdukan for children?

PDFdukan is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has shared personal data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

10. How will I know about changes?

When this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top changes too. Important changes, such as switching on advertising, will also be announced in the cookie notice.
