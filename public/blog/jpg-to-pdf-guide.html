Got a photo or screenshot you need to send as a PDF? You do not need any app. A free browser tool turns JPG images into clean PDF files in seconds.

Here is the thing: PDFs look the same on every device and are accepted everywhere. This guide shows you how to convert, and when PDF beats JPG.

Why convert JPG to PDF

Job forms, school work, and official uploads usually ask for PDF. A PDF keeps your layout fixed and bundles many images into one tidy file.

It also looks more professional. Sending a single PDF beats attaching ten loose photos that may open in a random order.

How to convert for free

Open the Image to PDF tool. Drag in your JPG or PNG files, set the page size, and click convert. Your PDF downloads right away.

Tip: Add several images at once to make a multi-page PDF in one go.

Tips for the best result

Start with a clear image. If the file is large, compress the image first so the final PDF stays small and easy to email.

Need text from the image too? Run it through OCR before converting. For details on formats, see JPEG and PDF on Wikipedia, and the W3C web standards site.
