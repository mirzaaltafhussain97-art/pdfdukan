What Does the Free Online OCR Tool Do?

PDFdukan's free online OCR image to text converter reads the words in a photo, screenshot or scanned PDF. It turns them into text you can edit. Tesseract OCR runs inside your browser, so your files are never uploaded. Copy the text or save it as TXT or Word.

OCR means optical character recognition. The tool finds letter shapes in the picture and turns them into characters you can search, edit and paste into an email or document.

You can extract text from image online free in 37 languages, including Spanish, Urdu, Arabic and Hindi, plus mixed English + Urdu pages. Each result shows a confidence estimate, a word count and a character count.

How Do I Extract Text From an Image?

Getting text out of an image takes five steps:

  1. Set the language. If the text isn't English, open Options and pick the Document language, such as English + Urdu.
  2. Add your files. Tap Choose Files, drop images or a PDF, or press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) to paste a screenshot.
  3. Match the layout. Simple two-column pages are read left column first on their own; if a newsletter's columns still mix, choose Two or more columns in Options and tap Re-extract.
  4. Wait a moment. Reading starts by itself; the first run downloads the OCR engine and takes longer.
  5. Check and save. Compare the text with the page shown beside it, fix any mistakes, then tap Copy or Export to save a Word or TXT file.

How Do I Get Accurate OCR Results?

Take the photo straight above the page in good light. Blur and shadows cause more errors than anything else. Crop away the table or background first if you can.

Pick the real language of the text, because Tesseract reads each script with its own model. Use Sparse text / screenshot for app screens with scattered labels.

When we tested six images, a phone photo of a lease, a receipt and a dim meeting note came back with almost no errors. A two-column page was only readable with Two or more columns.

Try Document contrast for faded paper, but compare it with Original pixels, because extra contrast can also hide thin strokes.

Are My Images Uploaded to a Server?

No. The OCR runs in your browser tab with Tesseract.js, so your images and PDFs stay on your device. The OCR code and language files are downloaded from a public CDN the first time you use a language.

Many free OCR sites send your file to their server and delete it later. Here there is nothing to delete, which matters for bank statements or medical forms. The page still loads analytics and ads.

What Can’t This OCR Tool Do?

Handwriting is not read reliably, because Tesseract is built for printed text. The tool gives you plain text, not a searchable PDF or an Excel table, and tables lose their grid.

Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first with Unlock PDF. HEIC and TIFF files are not accepted yet, so save them as JPG or PNG first.
