PDF to Word — Free Browser-Based Converter

Convert any PDF document to an editable Word file without installing software. Our tool uses PDF.js to accurately extract text content, preserving paragraph structure. The output is a .doc file compatible with Microsoft Word, LibreOffice, and Google Docs.

🔒
100% Private
Your PDF is processed entirely in the browser. No files are ever sent to any server.
📝
Editable Output
Download as .doc format — open directly in Word, LibreOffice, or Google Docs.
⚡
Instant Results
No upload wait times. Processing starts immediately in your browser.

⚠️ Important Note

This tool extracts text content from PDF files. Scanned PDFs (image-based) require OCR first — use our OCR Text Extraction tool. Complex layouts with tables, columns, or embedded fonts may have minor formatting differences in the output.
