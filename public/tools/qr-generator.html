About This Tool

This free QR code generator turns any link or text into a scannable QR code. It is perfect for sharing a website, a Wi-Fi password, a phone number, or a short note. The QR code is made right in your browser, so your data never leaves your device. Download the result as a high-quality PNG and use it on posters, cards, or screens.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your link or text in the box.
  2. Pick a size for your QR code.
  3. Click Generate QR Code.
  4. Click Download PNG to save it.
