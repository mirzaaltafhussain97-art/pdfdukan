What is the PDF to Excel Converter?

This tool extracts tables from a PDF and converts them into an Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheet, letting you edit, sort, and calculate the data.

Who needs this tool?

Finance professionals re-using PDF reports in Excel, data analysts extracting table data, and students working with PDF-based datasets.

What problem does it solve?

Data locked inside a PDF cannot be sorted, filtered, or calculated. This tool extracts the table data so you can work with it in Excel or Google Sheets.

How to Use

  3. Click the action button.
