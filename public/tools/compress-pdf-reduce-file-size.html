What Does the Compress PDF Tool Do?

PDFdukan's Compress PDF tool reduces PDF file size free, right in your browser. The default Keep text mode shrinks the photos and scans inside the PDF while text, links, form fields and bookmarks stay as they are. For the smallest file, Pages as images turns each page into a lighter JPEG.

You see the old and new size before you download. If the new file is not smaller, you get your original back instead of a bigger copy.

How Do I Compress a PDF?

Compressing a PDF takes four steps:

  1. Add your PDF. Tap Choose PDF or drop one file up to 200 MB.
  2. Pick a mode. Keep text for most files, or Pages as images for the strongest compression.
  3. Set the level. Light, Balanced or Strong, or a max size such as 500 KB.
  4. Compress and save. Tap Compress PDF, check the result, then Download PDF.

Keep Text or Pages as Images: Which Should I Use?

How Much Smaller Will My PDF Get?

Photos take far more space than text, so results vary. Our own tests on the Balanced level showed:

  • A 4-page scanned invoice, 3.55 MB, dropped to 199 KB (95% smaller).
  • A report with text and photos, 4.58 MB, dropped to 667 KB (86% smaller).
  • A 14-page text-only research paper, 992 KB, dropped to 856 KB (14% smaller).

Text-only files shrink the least, since text and vector drawings are already compact.

How Do I Get a PDF Under an Upload Limit?

Job portals, school forms and email often set a size limit. Choose Pages as images and tap a max size, from 100 KB to 5 MB. It tries sharper settings first. Gmail, for example, caps attachments on personal accounts at 25 MB.

If the limit is very small, the tool warns you when quality drops. Then remove pages you don't need with Extract PDF Pages, or send fewer pages at once.

Are My PDFs Uploaded?

No. Compress PDF reads and rebuilds your file with the open-source pdf-lib library running in your browser, so the PDF is not sent to a PDFdukan server. The page still loads site files, analytics and ads.
