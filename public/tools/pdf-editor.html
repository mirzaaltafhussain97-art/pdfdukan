What is the PDF Editor?

This tool lets you view a PDF and add annotations — text boxes, highlights, and drawings — directly on the page. The annotated PDF can be downloaded.

Who needs this tool?

Reviewers marking up documents, teachers annotating student work, and anyone who needs to add comments or corrections to a PDF without editing the original text.

What problem does it solve?

PDFs are not editable by default. This tool adds a layer of annotations on top of the existing content so you can mark up any PDF without changing the underlying document.

How to Use

  1. Upload your file.
  2. Choose your options.
  3. Click the action button.
  4. Download the result.
