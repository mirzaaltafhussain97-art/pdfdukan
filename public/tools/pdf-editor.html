What Is This Online PDF Editor?

PDFdukan is an online PDF editor, free with no sign up and without a watermark. You click a line of text and type over it, add text boxes, images, signatures, links and shapes, fill form fields, then download a PDF that keeps its original text sharp and searchable.

When you replace a line, PDFdukan removes the old words from the page itself and writes the new ones in a look-alike font of the same size, weight and color. Copy, search and screen readers then find only your new text, not a hidden copy under a white box.

Everything runs in your browser tab, with no account and no daily task limit.

How Do I Edit a PDF Online Without Signing Up?

Six steps take a PDF from opening to a finished download:

  1. Open the PDF. Click Open PDF or drop the file on the page; every page appears with its text ready to edit.
  2. Change existing text. Click any line, type the new words, then press Enter; the floating bar sets bold, italic, size and color.
  3. Add new content. Pick Text, Images, Links, Shapes, Draw or Annotate, then click the spot on the page where it belongs.
  4. Sign or fill. Choose Sign to draw, type or upload a signature, or click a form field and type your answer.
  5. Arrange pages. Above each page, rotate, move, delete or add a blank page after it.
  6. Download. Click Export PDF, name the file and save it; nothing is added to your pages.

What Can You Change in a PDF Here?

Each tool in the toolbar writes real PDF content, not a flat picture of the page:

Edit existing text

Retype names, dates or prices. Old words leave the file; new ones match the original font style.

Find and replace

Press Ctrl+F to find a word across all pages and replace one match or every match at once.

Sign, initial and date

Add a signature, initials, today’s date, a check or a cross, and reuse a saved signature next time.

Fillable form fields

Fill a PDF's own fields, or add text, multi-line, checkbox, radio and dropdown fields that others can fill.

Clickable links

Enter a web address or email, then size the link box over your text; it opens in any PDF reader.

Images, shapes and notes

Place a logo or photo, draw rectangles, circles, lines and arrows, sketch freehand or pin a sticky note.

Urdu, Arabic and symbols

Type right-to-left text or symbols like ≥ and ✓; Urdu in Naskh style stays searchable text.

Page tools

Rotate pages left or right, change their order, delete pages or insert blank ones for extra notes.

How Do I Sign a PDF Online for Free?

To sign a PDF online free, open it in PDFdukan, choose Sign, then pick Signature, Initials, Check, Cross or Date and click the spot on the page. Drag the signature to move it and pull a corner to resize it.

PDFdukan creates a signature three ways: draw it with a mouse or finger, type your name in one of 10 handwriting styles, or upload a photo of your signature with the paper made see-through. Ink comes in black, blue or red.

Tick “Save in this browser for next time” and your signature and initials appear as Saved buttons on your next visit. They stay in this browser’s storage only and are never uploaded; the ✕ on each button deletes it.

A signature added here is an electronic signature: a picture of your handwriting placed on the page. It is not a certificate-based digital signature, so check that the person asking for your signature accepts it.

How Do You Keep Edited Text Looking Like the Original?

PDFdukan reads each line's font name, size, weight and color, then writes the new words in an open font with the same widths. Times New Roman, Arial, Courier, Calibri and Cambria get close look-alikes, so a replaced line keeps its length and spacing. For new text you can pick from 43 fonts, including EB Garamond, Libre Baskerville, PT Sans, Roboto and fonts for Arabic, Urdu and Hindi.

Edit one line at a time and keep about the same number of letters, so the new words don't run into the next column. If a line belongs to a table cell, check the cell edge in the preview before you click Export PDF.

A logo font or handwriting font has no look-alike, so PDFdukan uses the nearest regular style. For a heading in such a font, place a text box instead and pick its size and color yourself.

Is My PDF Uploaded When I Edit It?

No. PDFdukan opens, edits and saves the PDF inside your browser, so the file never travels to our server and nothing is stored after you close the tab. Only the open-source PDF engines, Mozilla's pdf.js and pdf-lib, and the fonts load from public CDNs.

Cover works differently from text editing. Cover hides an area with a solid box, but words under that box can still be copied from the file. To take a word out for real, click the line and delete it, which removes it from the page.

What Can't This PDF Editor Do?

Scanned pages are pictures, so their words can't be retyped; run Searchable PDF first, or add a text box over the scan. Password-locked PDFs need Unlock PDF before they open here.

PDFdukan edits one line at a time, so whole paragraphs don't reflow like a word processor. For long rewrites, turn the file into a document with PDF to Word, edit it, then save it as PDF again.
