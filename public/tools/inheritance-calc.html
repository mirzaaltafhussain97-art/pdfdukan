📖 About Islamic Inheritance Law (Fara'id)

Fara'id is the Islamic law of inheritance derived from the Quran (Surah An-Nisa, 4:11-12, 4:176), Hadith, and scholarly consensus (Ijma). It establishes fixed fractional shares for specific heirs.

Mother's mandatory share (1/8): When the deceased leaves children, the mother receives one-eighth (1/8) of the total estate. This is a fixed Quranic obligation that must be deducted first before distributing the remainder.

2:1 Male-to-Female ratio: Among children, sons (brothers) receive double the share of daughters (sisters). This reflects the greater financial obligation placed on men under Islamic law to support women financially.

Note on scope: This calculator implements the core distribution between Mother, Brothers, and Sisters. A complete Fara'id calculation may also consider the Father (1/6), Husband/Wife, paternal grandparents, and other heirs. Consult a qualified Islamic scholar for complex estate situations.

Disclaimer: This tool is for educational and reference purposes only. Always verify with a qualified Islamic scholar (Mufti) and legal estate attorney for official proceedings.
