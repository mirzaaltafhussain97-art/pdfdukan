About This Tool

Have a long PDF and a quick question? Upload a PDF and ask anything about it. The text is read in your browser, then the AI answers based on its content — handy for contracts, manuals, and reports.

How to Use

  1. Upload your PDF.
  2. Type your question.
  3. Click Ask.
  4. Read the answer.
