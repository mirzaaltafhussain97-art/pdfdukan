Merge PDF Files Online — Free, Fast & Private

PDFdukan's Merge PDF tool combines two or more unlocked PDFs into one file. Choose pages from each source and arrange the file cards before export; selected pages remain in their original order within each source PDF. The merge operation runs in the browser, while the page can still request ordinary site assets, analytics and third-party libraries.

Whether you need to combine a cover letter with your CV, merge monthly bank statements into one document, or assemble scanned pages into a single report, this free online PDF merger handles it without any software installation, account registration, or file upload to a cloud server.

Why Use PDFdukan to Merge PDFs?

🔒 Client-side Merge

Selected files are read by the browser-based merge code. Assess your browser and network before handling highly sensitive documents.

📄 Choose Exact Pages

Select specific pages from each PDF, not just entire documents. Leave out cover pages, blank pages, or pages you don't need.

🔀 Drag to Reorder

Drag and drop files into the exact order you want before merging. No need to rename files or re-upload in sequence.

🆓 No Account Required

The current tool does not require an account or add a PDFdukan watermark. Large or complex files can exceed a device's available memory.

How to Merge PDFs in 4 Simple Steps

  1. Add your PDF files — Choose two or more unlocked PDFs. Capacity depends on total size, PDF complexity, browser and available memory.
  2. Arrange the order — Drag the file cards or use the ↑/↓ buttons. This controls which source document comes first.
  3. Select pages (optional) — Click page numbers, use All/None, or enter a validated range. Pages from each source keep their source order.
  4. Merge and verify — Click Merge PDFs, download the result, then open it and check page order, links, forms and fonts before submitting it.

Common Use Cases for Merging PDFs

  • Job applications: Combine your CV, cover letter, degree certificate, and experience letters into one clean PDF before submitting to a portal.
  • University admissions: HEC, universities, and scholarship portals often require a single PDF containing all documents.
  • Bank and legal documents: Merge multiple bank statements into one file for a loan application or visa process.
  • Scanned documents: If you scanned a multi-page document page by page, merge the individual PDFs into one complete file.
  • Invoices and receipts: Freelancers and small businesses can merge monthly invoices into one PDF for accounting or tax purposes.
