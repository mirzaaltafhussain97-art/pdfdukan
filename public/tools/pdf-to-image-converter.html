What Does the PDF to Image Converter Do?

PDFdukan's PDF to Image Converter lets you convert PDF to image online free. It turns each page into a JPG, PNG or WebP file at 72 to 600 DPI, right in your browser. It can also save the photos stored inside a PDF, and locked files open with their password.

Before you convert, the tool shows the exact pixel size of the first page. You pick only the pages you need, then download one image or a ZIP of numbered files, such as report-page-01.jpg.

How Do I Convert PDF to Image Online Free?

Converting a PDF to images takes five steps:

  1. Add your PDF. Drop one file or tap Choose PDF. Locked? Type its password and tap Open PDF.
  2. Select your pages. All pages start selected. Tap a page to skip it, or type a range like 1-3, 8.
  3. Choose what to save. Whole pages makes one image per page. Images inside the PDF saves the photos and logos on those pages.
  4. Set format and size. Pick JPG, PNG or WebP and a resolution from 72 to 600 DPI. Set Quality for JPG or WebP.
  5. Convert and download. Tap the Convert button, check the count and size, then tap Download. Several images come as one ZIP.

What Can You Do With This Tool?

These four features cover most PDF to image jobs:

Save whole pages

Each selected page becomes one image with its text and charts, ready for slides, posts or upload forms.

Pull out the pictures

Images inside the PDF extracts each photo, logo or scan at its stored size, without the text around it.

Open locked PDFs

Type the password once and a password-protected PDF opens in this tab. It is not saved or sent anywhere.

See the size first

The tool shows the pixel size before you convert and warns when a huge page must be drawn smaller.

When Should You Turn a PDF Into an Image?

Common reasons to save a PDF page as a picture:

  • Upload a signed form or ID page to a job or college portal that only accepts JPG.
  • Post a flyer on Instagram or Facebook, which won’t show a PDF.
  • Drop one slide from a report into PowerPoint or Google Slides.
  • Show a menu, price list or certificate on your website as a light WebP image.
  • Save product photos or charts from a catalog with Images inside the PDF.

How Do I Get Sharp, High-Quality Images From a PDF?

Match the DPI to the job: 150 for email and web pages, 200 for crisp small text, and 300 for printing.

Choose PNG for fine text or line charts, since JPG compression can blur sharp edges. Use JPG at 85–90% quality for photos or a smaller file size.

When we tested a 14-page research paper at 200 DPI, each page came out 1700 × 2200 px and the 14 JPGs totaled 11 MB. Need only part of a big page or map? Cut it out first with Crop PDF.

Is My PDF Uploaded to a Server?

No. The converter draws your PDF with Mozilla's pdf.js code in your browser tab, so the file never goes to a PDFdukan server. The page still loads site files, analytics and ads.

Because the work happens on your device, you can convert a bank statement or contract without handing it to a third party.

What This PDF to Image Tool Doesn’t Do

Text in a page image can’t be selected or edited, so keep the original PDF. The tool makes JPG, PNG and WebP only, not TIFF, and it converts one PDF at a time.

Images inside the PDF finds real pictures only, so use Whole pages for text, tables and vector charts. To share fewer pages as a PDF, use Extract PDF Pages.
