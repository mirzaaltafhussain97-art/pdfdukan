How the inheritance calculator works

This tool applies the fixed Quranic shares (faraid), then assigns the remaining estate to the residuary (asaba) heirs, and finally adjusts with Aul (when fixed shares exceed the estate) or Radd (when there is a surplus and no residuary). It also applies Hajb — the rules by which nearer heirs block more distant ones (for example, a son blocks grandchildren and siblings; the father blocks siblings and the grandfather).

Known limitations (consult a scholar)

  • Grandfather with siblings: the applicable Hanafi calculations and school differences need specialist review, so this combination is stopped.
  • Akdariyya (husband + mother + grandfather + one full sister) and Mushtaraka are special cases not separately modelled.
  • Remote generations: more distant grandfathers/grandchildren and dhawu al-arham require a scholar.
  • Pregnancy, missing persons, homicide, religion/nasab disputes and uncertain death order are deliberately stopped rather than estimated.
  • Pakistan law: a predeceased son/daughter branch requires a separate Section 4 calculation and is deliberately stopped here. The official text uses the wording “per stripes”; obtain professional advice on its application.
  • Ownership and Wasiyyah: joint ownership, gifts, nominations, disputed debts, and a bequest benefiting an heir or depending on consent are outside this calculator.
