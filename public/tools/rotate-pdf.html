Turn every page of your PDF by 90, 180, or 270 degrees. Fast, free, and fully private in your browser.

About This Tool

This free tool rotates all pages in a PDF at once. It is handy when scans come out sideways or upside down. The file is processed in your browser using pdf-lib, so it never leaves your device. Download the fixed PDF in seconds.

How to Use

  1. Upload your PDF file.
  2. Choose how far to rotate.
  3. Click Rotate & Download.
  4. Save the new PDF.
