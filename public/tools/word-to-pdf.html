Convert Word to PDF Online — Free, Fast & Private

PDFdukan's Word to PDF converter transforms any Microsoft Word document (.docx) into a PDF file instantly — preserving your headings, paragraphs, tables, images, and formatting exactly as they appear in Word. The conversion happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your document is never uploaded to any server, never stored in the cloud, and never seen by anyone else.

PDF is the universal format for sharing documents professionally. When you send a Word file, it can look different on the recipient's computer depending on which version of Word they have, which fonts are installed, and their page settings. Converting to PDF locks the layout permanently — your CV, report, assignment, or letter will look exactly the same on every device, from a phone screen to a printer.

Share contracts, proposals, and reports that cannot be accidentally edited by the recipient. PDF is the standard for formal business communication.

PPSC, FPSC, NTS, NADRA, and most government portals require applications and documents in PDF format with specific file size limits.

How to Convert Word to PDF in 3 Steps

  1. Upload your Word file — Click the upload area or drag and drop your .docx file. The file is loaded directly into your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
  2. Preview your document — The tool renders a preview of your Word document so you can confirm the content looks correct before converting.
  3. Download your PDF — Click the Convert to PDF button. Your PDF downloads instantly with all text, images, tables, and formatting preserved.

What Formatting Is Preserved?

  • Text formatting: Bold, italic, underline, font sizes, and paragraph styles are carried over to the PDF.
  • Tables: Tables with borders, merged cells, and text alignment are fully supported.
  • Images: Embedded images in your Word document appear in the PDF at their original positions.
  • Headings and structure: Heading 1, Heading 2, and paragraph styles are preserved in the PDF output.
  • Lists: Bulleted and numbered lists convert correctly with proper indentation.
  • Page layout: Page margins, orientation (portrait/landscape), and multi-column layouts are respected.

Why Convert Word to PDF Instead of Sharing the .docx?

Word files (.docx) can be edited by anyone who opens them. This is a risk for CVs, contracts, certificates, and any document where you need the content to stay exactly as you wrote it. PDF files cannot be easily edited without special software. Converting also ensures your document looks identical on every device — fonts, spacing, and layout will not shift regardless of what software the recipient uses.

Additionally, many portals and email systems only accept PDF. Government job applications, university submissions, visa applications, and most professional document workflows require PDF format. Converting from Word to PDF ensures your documents pass validation checks on these platforms.
