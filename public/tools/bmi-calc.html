BMI Calculator — What is Body Mass Index?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple measure of body fat based on height and weight. A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered normal. This free calculator supports metric (kg, cm) and imperial (lbs, ft/in) units and provides your category, ideal weight range, and a visual gauge.

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic measure. It does not account for muscle mass, bone density, age, or sex. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for personalised health advice.
