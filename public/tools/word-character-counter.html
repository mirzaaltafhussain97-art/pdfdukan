What Does the Word & Character Counter Do?

PDFdukan's free word & character counter online counts words, characters with and without spaces, sentences and paragraphs while you type or paste. It also estimates reading and speaking time, tracks a word or character limit, and lists your most-used words. Your text never leaves your browser.

How Do I Count Words and Characters?

Counting takes three steps:

  1. Add your text. Type or paste into Your Text. Every count updates as you go.
  2. Set a limit. Enter a Word limit or Character limit to see how much room is left.
  3. Review and copy. Check Top Words for repeats, then tap Copy text.

How Does the Counter Count?

What each number includes:

WordsAny text between two spaces or line breaks is one word, so "don't", "e-mail" and "1,000" each count once
CharactersLetters, numbers, punctuation and spaces, not line breaks; an emoji is one
SentencesEnd at . ! or ?; common abbreviations like "Dr." are skipped
ParagraphsEvery line that has text
Reading time238 words per minute, from a 2019 reading study by Marc Brysbaert
Speaking time183 words per minute, the same study's read-aloud rate

When Do People Check a Count?

Common reasons to check a count:

  • A college application essay with a word limit.
  • A meta description or social post with a character cap.
  • A cover letter that must fit one page.

Is My Text Saved or Uploaded?

No. The counter runs in your browser and keeps nothing, so refreshing the page clears your text.
