Image Compressor & Resizer — Free & Private

Resize or re-encode an image with exact pixel dimensions, presets, format and quality controls. An optional target-size search applies to lossy output, but the final size and visible quality depend on the source.

🗜️
Measured Size Comparison

Fits the full image without crop or distortion, centred inside a named canvas. Verify current platform requirements before upload.

Approximate Target Size
For JPG or WebP, the tool searches for the highest tested quality under the requested size when the encoder can reach it.
