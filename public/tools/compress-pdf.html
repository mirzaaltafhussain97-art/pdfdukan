What is the PDF Compressor?

PDFdukan's PDF Compressor reduces the file size of a PDF document while keeping the content readable. It works entirely in your browser — no file is uploaded to any server.

Who needs this tool?

Students sending assignments via email, professionals sharing reports, and anyone whose PDF is too large to upload to a government portal or email attachment limit.

What problem does it solve?

Large PDFs are rejected by email servers (Gmail limits attachments to 25MB), university portals, and job application forms. This tool shrinks PDFs so they pass any file-size limit.
