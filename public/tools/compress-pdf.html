How to Compress a PDF in 3 Easy Steps

  2. Choose compression level — Select the quality setting that matches your need. Use higher compression for email attachments, lower compression for documents you'll print or archive.
  3. Download your compressed PDF — Click the Compress button. Your reduced-size PDF is ready to download in seconds. The original file is untouched on your device.

When Should You Compress a PDF?

  • Email attachments: Gmail, Yahoo, and Outlook limit attachments to 25MB. Scanned documents often exceed this. Compress first, then attach.
  • University and job portals: Online application forms in Pakistan (HEC, FPSC, PPSC, bank job portals) typically cap uploads at 1–5MB. A compressed PDF ensures your application goes through.
  • WhatsApp and messaging apps: WhatsApp compresses documents automatically, sometimes degrading quality. Send a pre-compressed PDF to control the output quality yourself.
  • Website uploads: If you manage a website and upload PDFs — product catalogues, forms, reports — smaller files load faster and improve your Google PageSpeed score.
  • Shared drives and cloud storage: Smaller PDFs use less storage space in Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive.

How Much Does PDF Compression Reduce File Size?

The reduction depends on the content of your PDF. Image-heavy pages often have more room to shrink, but the result depends on page complexity and the selected quality tier. An already optimized file may not shrink; in that case PDFdukan returns the original bytes instead of a larger replacement.

The tool shows the original and final file sizes after processing. Because pages are rasterized, review small text and graphics in the downloaded PDF before submitting or printing it.
