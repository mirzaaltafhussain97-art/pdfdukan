Crop a PDF Region to a High-Quality Image — Free & Private

PDFdukan's Crop PDF tool lets you draw a box over part of the current PDF page and export that region as a JPG, PNG or WebP image. Instead of converting a whole page, you can keep one map area, stamp, plan detail or figure. Processing uses client-side PDF.js; the page can still request ordinary site assets, analytics and third-party libraries.

Cropping a smaller region can allow more output pixels than rendering an entire oversized page, but it cannot recover detail that is absent from the source. The tool automatically lowers the requested scale if an output would exceed its 8,000-pixel or 64-megapixel safety limits.

Why Use PDFdukan Crop PDF?

🔍 Measured Raster Output

The selected region is rendered from the PDF at the chosen scale, subject to the displayed browser-safe output cap.

🗺️ Made for Big Maps

Pull one area out of a huge GIS map or site plan that's otherwise too large to convert in full quality.

🖼️ JPG, PNG or WebP

PNG uses lossless image encoding; JPG and WebP use the selected quality. File size varies by content.

🔒 Client-Side File Processing

The selected PDF is opened and rendered locally with PDF.js rather than uploaded for server processing.

How to Crop a PDF in 3 Steps

  1. Upload your PDF — Drag and drop or click to browse. The first page appears as a preview you can select on.
  2. Select, rotate & add more — Drag a box over the area, fine-tune it with the corner & edge handles, and rotate it to any angle with the top handle. Click “Add crop” to mark several regions, or “Fit page” to grab the whole page.
  3. Pick quality and download — Choose format and resolution, then Download crop (or Download all as ZIP) to save your regions as high-quality images.

Common Uses

  • Maps & GIS: Extract a town, mauza, block or sector from a large map at full detail.
  • Engineering & architecture: Crop a single detail, elevation or title block from a big plan sheet.
  • Certificates & IDs: Save just a seal, signature or photo from a scanned document.
  • Reports & books: Pull one chart, table or figure out of a page to reuse elsewhere.
  • Social & messaging: Share a clear, cropped portion instead of a whole low-resolution page.
