Split PDF Online — Extract Pages Free & Private

PDFdukan's Split PDF tool divides an unlocked PDF into separate files: extract a custom page range such as 3–7, create one file for page 12, split every page, or make groups of a chosen size. The split operation runs in your browser; the page still requests ordinary site assets, analytics and PDF libraries from configured providers.

Long merged PDFs are common — a batch of contracts in one file, a complete e-book when you only need one chapter, or a 50-page scanned bundle where only two pages matter. Instead of sharing the entire document, split out exactly the pages you need in seconds.

Why Use PDFdukan to Split PDFs?

✂️ Custom Page Ranges

Extract any range — pages 3–7, page 12 only, or multiple ranges. You decide exactly what goes into the new PDF.

📑 Split Every Page

One click turns a 20-page PDF into 20 separate single-page PDFs, packaged neatly into a ZIP file for download.

🔒 Local File Processing

The selected PDF is processed by the page's client-side PDF code; confirm your browser and network conditions before using highly sensitive files.

🆓 No Account Required

The current tool does not require an account or add a PDFdukan watermark. Device memory still limits very large files.

How to Split a PDF in 3 Steps

  1. Upload your PDF — Click the upload area or drag and drop the PDF you want to split. Page thumbnails load instantly.
  2. Choose your split mode — Enter separate ranges such as 1–3, 5, 8–10; choose a group size; split every page; or click visual breakpoints on PDFs up to 60 pages.
  3. Download and verify — Download one result directly, pick individual parts, or save all multiple results in a ZIP. Check important links, forms, annotations and signatures after export.

Common Uses for Splitting PDFs

  • Extracting one document from a merged batch: HR departments and offices often receive many contracts or forms merged into one PDF — split out the one you need.
  • Sharing specific pages: Send only the relevant pages of a report instead of the entire 100-page file.
  • Separating scanned bundles: If multiple documents were scanned into a single PDF, split them back into individual files.
  • Removing confidential sections: Extract only the shareable pages and keep sensitive pages out of the file you send.
  • Study material: Students can extract a single chapter or topic from large course PDFs for focused reading and printing.
