Split PDF Online — Visual & Precise

PDFdukan's Split PDF tool gives you full control over how you divide your document. Choose a visual split mode to see all pages as thumbnails and click between them to set breakpoints, or use the classic modes: split every page, every N pages, or specify custom ranges like "1-3, 5, 8-10". All parts are packed into a ZIP. Nothing is uploaded.

About This Tool

This free tool lets you split a PDF into separate files or by page ranges. It is fast, easy, and works in your browser with no installation. There is no signup needed for basic use, and your privacy is respected.
