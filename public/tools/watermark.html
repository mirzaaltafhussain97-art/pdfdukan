What is the PDF Watermark Tool?

This tool adds a custom text watermark to every page of a PDF. You can control the text, colour, size, opacity, and position. No upload required.

Who needs this tool?

Businesses protecting confidential documents, photographers watermarking portfolios, educators stamping draft assignments, and anyone marking PDFs as "Confidential" or "Draft".

What problem does it solve?

Shared PDFs can be misused or distributed without permission. Adding a watermark signals ownership and discourages unauthorised redistribution.

How to Use

  1. Upload your file.
  2. Choose your options.
  3. Click the action button.
  4. Download the result.
