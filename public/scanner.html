CamMaster — Free AI Document Scanner Online

CamMaster by PDFdukan is a free, browser-based document scanner that turns photos of documents into clean, professional scans — without any app download. Point your phone camera at any document, upload the photo, and CamMaster automatically detects the document edges, corrects the perspective, removes shadows, and enhances the contrast to produce a sharp, readable scan.

The entire process runs in your browser using AI and WebGL — no photo is uploaded to any server. This makes CamMaster safe for scanning sensitive documents like CNICs, passports, salary slips, academic certificates, property papers, and medical reports. Your data stays completely on your device.

What Makes CamMaster Different from Other Scanners?

🤖 AI Edge Detection

An ONNX neural network running in your browser automatically finds document corners and corrects perspective — even for documents held at an angle.

📷 No App Needed

Works directly in your mobile browser — Chrome, Safari, Samsung Internet. No Play Store or App Store download required. Open the website and scan immediately.

📄 Batch Scanning

🔒 100% Private

Photos never leave your device. AI processing, enhancement, and PDF export all happen locally. No cloud, no server, no privacy risk.

How to Scan a Document with CamMaster

  1. Take a photo or upload an image — Use your phone camera to photograph the document, or upload an existing photo from your gallery. Place the document on a flat, dark surface with good lighting for best results.
  2. Auto-detect and crop — CamMaster's AI automatically detects the four corners of your document and applies perspective correction to remove any tilt or angle. You can also adjust the crop corners manually.
  3. Enhance the scan — Apply the auto-enhance filter to increase contrast, remove shadows, and make text sharper. Choose between colour, greyscale, or black-and-white output.
  4. Export as PDF or JPG — Download your scan as a high-quality PDF (ideal for submitting documents) or JPG image. For multi-page documents, all pages are combined into one PDF.

Best Uses for CamMaster Scanner in Pakistan

  • CNIC scanning: Scan both sides of your National Identity Card for online applications, bank account openings, and government portals.
  • Educational documents: Scan degree certificates, transcripts, and mark sheets for university applications, HEC attestation, and scholarship forms.
  • Job applications: Scan experience letters, appointment letters, and salary slips for CV submission and employment verification.
  • Legal and property documents: Create clean, readable scans of property papers, tenancy agreements, and court documents.
  • Bank documents: Scan cheques, bank statements, and account opening forms for loan applications and financial processes.
  • Student assignments: Photograph handwritten assignments page by page and export as a single multi-page PDF for submission.

Tips for the Best Scan Quality

  • Place the document on a dark or contrasting background — a dark table or floor helps the AI find document edges accurately.
  • Use natural daylight or bright indoor light. Avoid direct flash which creates glare and washes out text.
  • Hold the camera directly above the document, not at an angle — the perspective correction works best with less tilt to correct.
  • Make sure the entire document is within the frame — don't crop off corners before uploading.
  • For very old or faded documents, use the enhance filter to boost contrast and make text readable.
