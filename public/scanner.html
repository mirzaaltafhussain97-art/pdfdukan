CamMaster by PDFdukan is a free online document scanner that uses OpenCV.js computer vision to automatically detect document edges in your photos. Upload any image taken with your phone or camera — CamMaster identifies the four corners of your document and applies perspective correction so the output looks like it was photographed straight on, perfectly flat. Choose from nine scan filters including Magic Pro (shadow removal), Black & White (crisp text), and Auto-Enhance (general improvement). Combine multiple scanned pages into a single multi-page PDF. Supports batch import of multiple images and full PDF rendering. All processing runs entirely in your browser — your documents never leave your device, keeping every file completely private and secure.
