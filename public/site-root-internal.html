Are Free Online PDF Tools Safe to Use?

Free online PDF tools are safe when your file never leaves your control. Many sites upload your PDF to their server and delete it later; PDFdukan reads it in your browser instead, using open-source engines such as pdf.js, pdf-lib and qpdf.

The trade-off: long scans use your phone’s memory. See our Privacy Policy.
