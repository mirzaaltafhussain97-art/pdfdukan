5 Best Free CamScanner Alternatives in 2026

Written and checked by the PDFdukan Product Team. PDFdukan owns CamMaster; that relationship is disclosed so you can judge our recommendation fairly.

Quick answer

The best CamScanner alternative depends on where you want your scans stored. Use CamMaster when you want a no-install browser scanner with local core processing and no visible watermark on the basic export; Adobe Scan for OCR and Acrobat integration; Google Drive for searchable PDFs saved directly to Drive; Apple Notes or Files for built-in iPhone and iPad scanning; and OneDrive for Microsoft cloud workflows.

Microsoft Lens is not included as a current recommendation because Microsoft retired the standalone app in 2026 and directs users to OneDrive instead.

Which alternative should you choose?

1. CamMaster — best no-install browser option

CamMaster runs from a browser and provides edge detection, corner adjustment, perspective correction, enhancement filters, page reordering, multi-page PDF export, high-quality JPG and lossless PNG output. The basic scanner does not require an account or add a visible PDFdukan watermark.

  • Choose it for: quick scans on a shared or low-storage device.
  • Privacy model: core scan processing is designed to run locally; Google Drive is optional.
  • Limitation: results depend on camera quality, lighting, contrast, and device performance.

Open CamMaster scanner · Read PDFdukan privacy details

2. Adobe Scan — best for Acrobat and OCR workflows

Adobe describes automatic boundary detection, perspective correction, glare and shadow cleanup, OCR, PDF/JPEG output, and Acrobat integration. Adobe also states that its scan workflow uses an online service and Adobe cloud storage.

  • Choose it for: searchable PDFs and an existing Acrobat workflow.
  • Limitation: advanced PDF handling may require an Acrobat subscription.

Adobe Scan official page

3. Google Drive — best for searchable PDFs in Drive

Google says the Drive mobile app can scan receipts, letters, and billing statements and save them as searchable PDFs in Google Drive. Google also notes that the Drive web version does not provide the scanner.

  • Choose it for: users who already organize documents in Google Drive.
  • Limitation: the scan is part of a cloud-storage workflow.

Google Drive scanning help

4. Apple Notes or Files — best built-in iPhone option

Apple documents automatic page-boundary capture, manual corner adjustment, filters, multiple pages, PDF saving, and signatures through Notes. Files can scan directly into a folder you choose.

  • Choose it for: iPhone or iPad users who want a built-in workflow.
  • Limitation: it is tied to Apple's device ecosystem.

Apple scanning guide

5. OneDrive — best Microsoft Lens replacement

Microsoft retired Lens in 2026 and recommends the OneDrive scanner. OneDrive supports document, whiteboard, business-card, and photo capture with crop, rotate, filters, and PDF saving.

  • Choose it for: users already storing work in OneDrive.
  • Limitation: Microsoft says scans cannot be saved locally in the Lens-replacement workflow.

Microsoft Lens retirement notice

Privacy, OCR and offline questions

Which option keeps the core scan on my device?

CamMaster is the clearest option in this list when your priority is local core processing and a local download. Optional Drive saving still sends the file to Google when you deliberately use that feature. Apple Files can save to a location you choose, but whether that folder syncs to cloud storage depends on your device setup.

Which option is best for OCR?

Adobe Scan and Google Drive explicitly document searchable-text workflows. PDFdukan provides a separate browser OCR tool and a searchable PDF tool. OCR accuracy always depends on image sharpness, language, font, lighting, and page layout.

Can I use these without an app?

CamMaster runs in a browser. Adobe Scan, Google Drive scanning, Apple Notes/Files, and OneDrive scanning are mobile-app or operating-system workflows.

Which CamScanner alternative works without a watermark?

CamMaster's basic PDF, JPG and PNG exports currently do not add a visible PDFdukan watermark. Other providers can change free-tier limits and watermark rules, so confirm their current terms before committing to a workflow.

Is CamMaster the same as CamScanner?

No. CamMaster is PDFdukan's independent browser-based scanner. CamScanner is a separate product and trademark owned by its respective company. This page compares documented workflows; it does not claim affiliation.

How this comparison was researched

We selected options that currently provide a documented document-scanning workflow and represent different user needs: browser-local, OCR/Acrobat, Google cloud, Apple built-in, and Microsoft cloud. We checked official product or support pages on 24 August 2026. We did not assign fabricated scores, repeat unverifiable marketing claims, or treat an app-store listing as proof of privacy. Features can change, so confirm the official source before choosing a service for sensitive documents.
