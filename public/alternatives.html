5 Best Free CamScanner Alternatives in 2026

By the PDFdukan Product Team. PDFdukan owns CamMaster, one of the tools below. We disclose that so you can judge our picks fairly. Main facts checked against official product and support pages on 9 November 2026; other-app prices come from third-party reviews and vary by region.

The 5 best free CamScanner alternatives in 2026 are CamMaster, Genius Scan, Adobe Scan, Google Drive, and Apple Notes or Files. CamMaster runs in a browser with no install, while the other four are phone apps that differ in OCR, ads, and storage.

The real difference is where each scan ends up after you tap save.

Why Do People Look for a CamScanner Alternative?

People look for a CamScanner alternative mainly because the free plan adds a watermark to scans and locks full OCR behind Premium.

Privacy is the second reason. In 2019, Kaspersky found a malicious Trojan-Dropper in an ad SDK inside the CamScanner Android app. Google pulled the app from Google Play after the report.

CamScanner also uploads scans to its own cloud, and it has faced government bans in some countries. That matters most when the page is a passport, a contract, or a financial statement.

CamMaster: Scanning in Your Browser With No Install

CamMaster is PDFdukan's free web-based scanner, so it works on a phone, laptop, or shared office PC without installing anything. The basic scanner needs no account and adds no visible watermark to exports.

Upload a photo or a PDF, and automatic edge detection finds the page while perspective correction straightens it. You can drag the corner handles, apply the Magic Pro filter or switch to B&W, then reorder or rotate pages.

CamMaster exports a multi-page PDF in A4, Letter, or A3, or a JPG. Core processing runs locally in your browser, and the file downloads straight to your device.

CamMaster's results depend on your camera, lighting, and device speed. Because it is a web tool, people who scan on the go all day may prefer a phone app.

Genius Scan: A Free App Without Watermarks

Genius Scan suits people who want a real phone app without a watermark on every page. Genius Scan's help center says the free Basic plan has no document limit and adds no watermarks.

Genius Scan works without an account and supports batch scanning. Text recognition is the gap: the App Store listing marks OCR and searchable PDFs as premium features.

Adobe Scan: Strong OCR, Tied to Adobe's Cloud

Adobe Scan is the free pick when searchable PDFs matter more than storage control. Adobe says the app is free to download and includes OCR, and it pairs image correction with Acrobat.

Adobe Scan stores scans in Adobe Document Cloud, though you can also save locally or to Dropbox or Google Drive. Advanced tasks, such as combining files, need Acrobat Pro.

Google Drive: Searchable PDFs Saved Straight to Drive

The Google Drive scanner lives inside the Drive mobile app, so Drive users need nothing extra. Google's steps are short: tap the Camera button, let the blue outline mark the crop, and save a searchable PDF or JPG.

Every Drive scan lands in your Google account, and Google's help page covers scanning only in the mobile app.

Apple Notes or Files: The Built-In iPhone Option

Apple Notes is the built-in scanner on iPhone and iPad, and it costs nothing. In Notes, tap the camera icon and choose Scan Documents; the phone finds the edges and saves a PDF.

Live Text lets you copy or search words in an Apple scan, and iOS 26 added a Preview app with its own scanner. Files can scan straight into a folder you choose, and whether that folder syncs to cloud storage depends on your settings.

What Happened to Microsoft Lens?

Microsoft Lens is no longer an option, because Microsoft retired the app in 2026. Retirement began January 9, 2026; on February 9, support ended and the app left the stores. New scans stopped on March 9.

Microsoft now points former Lens users to the OneDrive scanner. OneDrive is cloud-first, and Microsoft's notice says it does not support saving scans locally.

What Does a "Free" Scanner App Actually Leave Out?

A free scanner app often leaves out OCR, clean exports, or ad-free use, even when the download costs nothing. Several apps behave like trials in disguise until you subscribe.

The usual advice is to pick the app with the most features, but that rarely fits a free user. Test the full flow, from scan to edit, export, and share, before trusting any free app with real work.

Receipts and warranties need one extra check: can you find a scan later by merchant, date, or amount? An unnamed PDF is useless for reimbursement or tax records.

Which CamScanner Alternative Is Safe for Sensitive Documents?

The safest CamScanner alternative for sensitive documents processes and stores scans on your own device. The scan itself is not the risk; uploading every page to a company's servers by default is.

Apple's built-in tools and Scanner Pro keep scans on the device. Adobe Scan, iScanner, and CamScanner send scans to cloud storage or their own servers by default.

You can check any browser scanner's privacy claim yourself with a simple offline test:

  1. Open the scanner page and let it load fully.
  2. Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  3. Scan and export a non-sensitive test page.
  4. If the export still works, the processing really runs on your device.

Run this test before you scan an ID, a contract, or a medical record. On a shared computer, close the tab afterward, since local processing cannot protect an unlocked device. For a business, cloud uploads also raise compliance questions under another country's data laws.

How Do You Pick the Right Scanner App?

Pick a scanner app by matching it to your platform, how often you need OCR, and where you want files stored. These questions settle most choices quickly:

  • Scanning on a shared or low-storage device? A browser tool avoids installing anything.
  • Need searchable text often? Adobe Scan or a paid OCR tier fits better.
  • Handling private records? Choose on-device processing.
  • Scanning every day? Batch mode and organizing features save real time.

For text in a scan you already have, PDFdukan's OCR tool reads it in the browser. Pay for an app only when you need faxing, e-signatures, or multi-language OCR. For capture tips, read how to scan documents on your phone for free.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free CamScanner alternative with no watermark?

The best free CamScanner alternative with no watermark is CamMaster in a browser or Genius Scan as a phone app. CamMaster's basic exports carry no visible watermark. Genius Scan's free Basic plan has no watermarks and no document limit. Apple Notes and Google Drive are watermark-free too.

Is there a CamScanner alternative without ads or a subscription?

Yes, several CamScanner alternatives work without ads or a subscription. Genius Scan's Basic plan is free with no subscription, and FairScan is fully free, open-source, and ad-free on Android. TurboScan avoids a subscription with a $12.99 one-time price.

Is CamScanner safe to use?

Use CamScanner with caution: it suits low-sensitivity pages but uploads scans to its own cloud. Its Android app carried malware in a third-party ad library in 2019, later removed. For confidential files, use an on-device scanner.

Which of the 5 best free CamScanner alternatives in 2026 works without an app?

CamMaster is the only pick on this list that works without an app. CamMaster runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets, and computers. Adobe Scan, Genius Scan, Google Drive, and Apple Notes all need a mobile app or the phone's own system.

Do I need OCR in a scanner app?

You need OCR only if you want to search, copy, or edit the text in a scan. Apple's Live Text and Google Drive make scans searchable for free. Genius Scan keeps OCR in its paid tier.

Try a scan before you decide

Use a non-sensitive sample page to check edge detection, crop control, readability, export quality, and speed on your own device.
