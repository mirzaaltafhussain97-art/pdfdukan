Our Story

PDFdukan was founded with a single belief: essential document tools should be free, fast, and completely private. Too many people are forced to upload sensitive documents — pay stubs, contracts, IDs — to unknown servers just to perform a basic conversion. We built PDFdukan to change that.

Everything on PDFdukan runs entirely inside your web browser. Your files never leave your device. We built the entire processing stack — scanning, cropping, enhancement, PDF generation, merging, splitting, OCR — using browser-native APIs and open-source libraries. No server ever sees your data.

CamMaster — Our Flagship Scanner

CamMaster is our AI-powered document scanner. It uses OpenCV.js computer vision to automatically detect document edges, apply perspective correction, and enhance scan quality in real time — all inside your browser. Think CamScanner, but private and free forever.

What We Offer

  • 📷 Smart Scan — AI edge detection, perspective warp, shadow removal
  • 📑 Image to PDF — Convert JPG, PNG, WEBP to multi-page PDFs
  • 🖼️ PDF to JPG — Extract every page as a high-quality image
  • 🔗 Merge PDF — Combine PDFs, choose specific pages per file
  • ✂️ Split PDF — Visual breakpoint splitting and custom ranges
  • 🗜️ Compress Image — Reduce file sizes without noticeable quality loss
  • 🔤 OCR Text — Extract text from images using Tesseract.js
  • 📅 Age Calculator — Precise age computation in multiple units

Our Privacy Promise

Zero uploads. Zero tracking. Zero data collection. All file processing happens 100% in your browser using JavaScript. We do not have the ability to access, store, or view any document you process through PDFdukan.

We use only essential cookies (theme preference, recent docs list stored in your localStorage). No analytics tracking beyond anonymous page views. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Open Technology Stack

PDFdukan is built on industry-standard open-source libraries:

  • PDF-lib — PDF creation, merge, and split
  • PDF.js — PDF rendering and page preview
  • OpenCV.js — Computer vision for edge detection
  • Tesseract.js — Optical Character Recognition
  • jsPDF — Image-to-PDF generation
  • JSZip — Client-side ZIP creation
