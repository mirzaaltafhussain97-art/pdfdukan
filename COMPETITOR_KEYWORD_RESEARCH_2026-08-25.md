## PDFdukan comparison

PDFdukan's `scanner.html` contains approximately **1,684 visible words**, one H1 and 14 H2 headings after the current audit. It is already deeper than the reviewed first-page candidates by raw word count. Adding generic paragraphs merely to exceed competitors would increase duplication and weaken intent.

The stronger path is:

1. Keep the actual scanner above the fold.
2. State automatic-detection limitations next to the feature, not only in legal copy.
3. Use one primary scanner intent and support it with tightly related sub-intents.
4. Demonstrate output formats and verification steps without unverifiable accuracy or privacy absolutes.
5. Link scanner users to OCR, Searchable PDF, Image to PDF, Merge PDF and Image Compressor based on the next task they need.

## Observed topic and feature gaps

### Valuable topics to retain or strengthen

- Online document scanner
- Scan photo to PDF
- Document photo scanner
- Camera document scanner
- Multi-page scan to PDF
- Crop and perspective correction
- Readability filters / black-and-white scan
- JPG, PNG, WebP and PDF input distinctions
- PDF, JPG, PNG and ZIP output distinctions
- OCR text extraction as a separate workflow
- ID/CNIC, receipt, assignment and office-document use cases
- Mobile browser limits, memory limits and output verification

### Competitor wording not worth copying

- “95–99% OCR accuracy” without a reproducible benchmark
- “Perfect” or “CamScanner-quality” output claims
- “Any device,” “all platforms,” “instant,” or “file size does not matter” absolutes
- “Nothing is uploaded” wording that ignores analytics, CDN assets and optional connected actions
- Repeating the exact-match keyword unnaturally in every paragraph

PDFdukan's current audited copy deliberately avoids these claims.
